## DEFINITIONS

[FACT][LAW-DEF-010] `algocracy` = governance where sanction severity is computed primarily from quantified damage / restitution value, under bounded legal procedures.
[FACT][LAW-DEF-020] `restitution_value` = legally accepted accounting of loss (material, medical, productivity, replacement, recovery externalities), used for sanction tiering.
[FACT][LAW-DEF-030] `correctional_debt` = legal obligation to repay restitution_value through constrained economic participation (labor + privilege constraints).
[FACT][LAW-DEF-040] `insolvency` = legal state where computed restitution_value exceeds the subject’s projected lifetime earning capacity under baseline constraints.

## INVARIANTS

[DECISION][LAW-INV-010] Scenario A baseline uses value-generating correction; passive confinement is not the mass default instrument.
[DECISION][LAW-INV-020] Sanction model is damage-first: legal reasoning is framed around quantified loss and recovery feasibility, not ritualized retribution.
[DECISION][LAW-INV-030] Tier escalation is driven by (a) restitution_value magnitude, (b) ability-to-pay, (c) public safety risk classification, (d) evidence sufficiency.
[DECISION][LAW-INV-040] Coercive medical/biological instruments (e.g., pharmacological dampers) are permitted only under explicitly bounded procedures.

## CONTENT

### 1. Crime Typology (Damage Hierarchy)

[STATE][LAW-CRIME-010] crime.tier_1 = "protocol_violations" (minor public-order / compliance violations; low restitution_value).
[STATE][LAW-CRIME-020] crime.tier_2 = "resource_and_quota_offenses" (theft, fraud, vandalism, regulated smuggling; medium restitution_value).
[STATE][LAW-CRIME-030] crime.tier_3 = "high_damage_offenses" (severe bodily harm, homicide, large-scale sabotage/arson, catastrophic safety violations; high restitution_value).

### 2. Tier 1 Sanctions (Automated Micro-Correction)

[STATE][LAW-SAN-010] tier_1.response_mode = "automated_fine__instant_debit_or_quota_adjustment".
[STATE][LAW-SAN-020] tier_1.impact_profile = ["micro_debit", "temporary_luxury_quota_reduction", "service_throttling_limited"].
[STATE][LAW-SAN-030] tier_1.record_policy = "expunge_on_settlement" (no persistent penalty after settlement under baseline conditions).

### 3. Tier 2 Sanctions (Correctional Debt)

[STATE][LAW-SAN-040] tier_2.trigger = "damage_exceeds_instant_liquidity" OR "repeat_pattern_under_window".
[STATE][LAW-SAN-050] tier_2.instruments = ["debt_assignment", "privilege_block_limited", "garnishment_rules", "mobility_constraints_situational"].
[STATE][LAW-SAN-060] tier_2.detention_default = "non_custodial" (home/work participation preserved where feasible).
[STATE][LAW-SAN-070] tier_2.minimums = ["basic_caloric_minimum", "basic_housing_minimum", "medical_minimum"].
[STATE][LAW-SAN-080] tier_2.economic_rule = "garnish_excess_income_first" (baseline allows near-total capture of discretionary/excess share).

### 4. Tier 3 Sanctions (Insolvency Protocol)

[STATE][LAW-SAN-090] tier_3.trigger = "restitution_value > projected_lifetime_earnings".
[STATE][LAW-SAN-100] tier_3.status_label = "insolvency__ward_of_state" (legal dependency state).
[STATE][LAW-SAN-110] tier_3.assignment_mode = "indefinite_high_yield_duty__bounded_by_safety_regimes".
[STATE][LAW-SAN-120] tier_3.logic = "only_high_yield_work_services_insolvency" (interest/maintenance of debt requires elevated yield categories).

### 5. Correction Instruments (Neuro-Correction)

[STATE][LAW-CORR-010] neuro_correction.permitted = true.
[STATE][LAW-CORR-020] neuro_correction.baseline_default = false.
[STATE][LAW-CORR-030] neuro_correction.applicability = "impulse_violence_risk_class" AND "due_process_completed".
[STATE][LAW-CORR-040] neuro_correction.mode = "pharmacological_damper" (aggression suppression as side-effect constrained instrument).
[STATE][LAW-CORR-050] neuro_correction.oversight = "human_tribunal_required".
[STATE][LAW-CORR-060] neuro_correction.must_be_additive = true (may accompany debt; does not replace restitution accounting).

### 6. Prison Status (Mass Baseline)

[STATE][LAW-PRSN-010] prison.mass_default = "abolished" (passive incarceration is not the baseline mass instrument).
[STATE][LAW-PRSN-020] custody.exceptional_use = "situational_short_term" (temporary isolation for acute safety containment, not long-term default).
